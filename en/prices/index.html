Tattoo prices in Wieluń – how we quote

Every tattoo is different, so we don't publish an off-the-shelf price list. We give a reliable price once we know the design.

What affects the price of a tattoo

Size

A larger area means more working time.

Level of detail and style

Realism and dense shading take more time than simple linework.

Placement

Some areas are technically harder (e.g. ribs, hands, knees).

Number of sessions

Large projects are done in stages.

Custom design

Time spent preparing the design is part of the service.

How the quote process works

  1. Send an enquiry – a description of the design, placement, approximate size in cm and inspiration photos.
  2. Preliminary quote – we give an approximate range or explain how it will be charged. The final cost is set individually based on the design and working time.
  3. Consultation – we fine-tune the details.
  4. Deposit and date – your booking is confirmed by paying a deposit within 7 days of booking; without it, the date is cancelled automatically. The deposit is non-refundable, tied to one person and one date, and deducted from the price of the tattoo at final payment. Deposit: 100 PLN for a small tattoo, 200 PLN for a large one, or set individually.
  5. Session – payment after the session, minus the deposit. Payment: BLIK or cash.

Why the cheapest offer isn't worth it

A tattoo stays for life. Touch-ups, cover-ups or tattoo removal usually cost more than a tattoo done well the first time.

Gift vouchers

Yes – we offer personalised gift vouchers. A voucher is valid for 1 year from the date of purchase, cannot be transferred or resold to another person, and one voucher can be used per session.

Book a consultation

Have an idea, or just a general direction? Message us: describe the design, placement and approximate size, and attach your inspirations. We'll get back to you and suggest a consultation date.
