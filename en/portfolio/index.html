A portfolio is the most honest calling card a studio has. Here you'll see work done at SHADY TATTOO – both fresh and healed, because a healed tattoo shows its true quality.

How to look at a portfolio

Look at detail and gradients

In realism and black & grey, what matters is smooth shading, contrast and readability from a distance.

Look for healed work

A photo straight after the session is one thing – the tattoo a few weeks later is another.

Categories

Fine line

Delicate, thin lines and subtle, minimalist designs – fine line is one of the styles we do at the studio.

Inspiration, not a copy

Our portfolio shows our style and what we can do. We don't repeat designs made for other clients one-to-one – your tattoo will be designed from scratch.
