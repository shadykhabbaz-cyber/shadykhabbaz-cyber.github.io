Black & grey tattoos in Wieluń

Black & grey is tattooed with black ink and its dilutions. No colour – instead, a full range of greys, from deep black to delicate, almost translucent gradients.

What makes black & grey special

Timeless

The understated look goes with any style and ages well.

Depth through shading

Grey wash and smooth transitions create a three-dimensional effect.

Versatile

It works for realism, religious motifs, ornaments, lettering and compositions.

Black & grey and realism

The two styles often go together: a realistic portrait in greys is one of the most popular kinds of tattoo. Read more on the realistic tattoos page, and see finished black & grey work from Wieluń in our portfolio.

How grey tattoos age

Good contrast and well-planned light areas (often bare skin left untouched) keep the tattoo readable. Sun protection significantly extends how long it looks its best.

Who it's for

For people who value elegance and subtlety and want a tattoo that won't go out of fashion.
