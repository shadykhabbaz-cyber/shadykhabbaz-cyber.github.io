ONE LIFE. ONE SKIN. ONE RIGHT CHOICE.

A tattoo stays with you for life, so we treat every project as a decision you make once – and make well. At SHADY TATTOO, ul. Głęboka 15/3, 98-300 Wieluń, Poland, we create realistic tattoos, black & grey, fine line and designs drawn from scratch for one specific person.

Why SHADY TATTOO

A design made just for you

We don't copy other people's work. Every design is based on a conversation, your story and the anatomy of the spot where it will sit.

Sterile and safe

We work with single-use needles and sterile tools, following the hygiene standards that apply to tattoo studios.

Trusted by clients

Rated 5.0 on Google (as of October 2026).

Registered business

Registered in the Polish business register (CEIDG) since August 2023.

What we specialise in

Realistic tattoos

Black & grey

Custom designs

From an idea to a finished design that fits your body.

First time?

We've put together a first tattoo guide, tips on how to prepare and how to care for your tattoo.

How it works

  1. Get in touch – describe your idea and send inspirations.
  2. Consultation – we agree on style, size, placement and a preliminary quote.
  3. Design – we prepare the design and make any changes.
  4. Session – we tattoo in a calm, sterile environment.
  5. Healing – you get aftercare instructions and support after the session.

Book a consultation

Have an idea, or just a general direction? Message us: describe the design, placement and approximate size, and attach your inspirations. We'll get back to you and suggest a consultation date.
