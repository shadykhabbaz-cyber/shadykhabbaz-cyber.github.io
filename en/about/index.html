About SHADY TATTOO, Wieluń

ONE LIFE. ONE SKIN. ONE RIGHT CHOICE.

This slogan isn't an advertising line – it's how we work. You have one skin, and it deserves a well-thought-out design, careful execution and an honest conversation, even when that means talking you out of a bad idea.

Our story

SHADY TATTOO is based in Wieluń, Poland, and the business has been registered in the Polish business register (CEIDG) since August 2023.

Who tattoos

At SHADY TATTOO, tattoos are done by Shady (Shady Khabbaz, the studio owner), Ola and Zuzia. Martyna, our studio manager, handles bookings and organisation.

Shady

The studio owner. Besides tattooing, he designs original graphics – such as the studio's hoodie design.

Zuzia

Part of the team since November 2025. Minimalist designs are her speciality, with a focus on delicate, floral motifs. She tattoos at weekends (hours by arrangement).

Martyna

Our studio manager, who takes care of absolutely everything – from paperwork to piercing. She performs the piercings herself, including ear piercing with the STUDEX System75. Our work includes realistic tattoos, fine line, cover-ups and custom designs; we also offer piercing at the studio.

The owner also runs a separate business in a different industry – Happy Kids Studio.

Our values

Honesty

We advise on size, placement and style so your tattoo looks good for years – even if that means changing the original idea.

Individuality

Every design is created for one specific person.

Safety

Sterility and single-use materials are the standard, not an extra.

A calm atmosphere

No rushing – especially with a first tattoo.
