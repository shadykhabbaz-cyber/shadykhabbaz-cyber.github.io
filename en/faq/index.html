FAQ – frequently asked questions

Short, clear answers to the questions we hear most often.

About the studio

Where is SHADY TATTOO located?

SHADY TATTOO is in Wieluń, Poland, at ul. Głęboka 15/3, 98-300 Wieluń.

What styles does SHADY TATTOO work in?

SHADY TATTOO specialises in realistic tattoos, black & grey, fine line (delicate, thin lines) and custom designs drawn from scratch. See examples in the portfolio.

What are SHADY TATTOO's reviews like?

SHADY TATTOO is rated 5.0 on Google (as of October 2026).

How long has SHADY TATTOO been in business?

SHADY TATTOO has been registered in the Polish business register (CEIDG) since August 2023.

What are the opening hours?

Monday–Friday 9:00–19:00. At weekends, Zuzia and Ola tattoo by individual arrangement. All visits are by appointment.

Prices and booking

How much does a tattoo cost at SHADY TATTOO?

The price depends on size, level of detail, placement and number of sessions. We quote individually once you send a description of your idea, the placement, approximate size and inspirations. See how it works on the prices page.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Your booking is confirmed by paying a deposit within 7 days of booking; without it, the date is cancelled automatically. The deposit is non-refundable, tied to one person and one date, and deducted from the price of the tattoo at final payment. Deposit: set individually. You can reschedule once, no later than 3 days before the visit. Payment: BLIK or cash.

Do you charge for a consultation or quote?

No – consultations and quotes are free. Just send a description of your idea or a screenshot of an inspiration.

How do I book a tattoo?

Send a message via the contact form or social media describing your idea, placement and size; we'll suggest a consultation and a date.

Before your tattoo

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

A tattoo usually causes discomfort often compared to scratching or burning; how intense it feels depends on the placement and your individual sensitivity.

How old do I need to be to get a tattoo?

We only tattoo adults (18+). If we suspect someone is underage, the studio may refuse the service, so please bring an ID.

How should I prepare for a tattoo?

Get a good night's sleep, eat a meal, stay hydrated and avoid alcohol beforehand; tell your artist about any medical conditions and medication. Full checklist: how to prepare for a tattoo; planning your first tattoo? See our guide.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes, inspirations are welcome; we use them to create a custom design that fits your body, rather than copying someone else's work.

Can you tattoo a portrait from a photo?

Yes, we do portrait tattoos from a sharp, high-resolution photo with clear lighting.

After your tattoo

How long does a tattoo take to heal?

The surface usually heals in about 2–4 weeks; the deeper layers of skin take longer to regenerate.

How do I care for a fresh tattoo?

Wash it gently, apply a thin layer of the recommended product, don't scratch it, and avoid sun, swimming pools and saunas until it has healed. Details: tattoo aftercare.

Do you do touch-ups and cover-ups?

Yes, we do cover-ups (covering old tattoos), as well as refreshing and removing tattoos. Report any justified concerns about the tattoo within 14 days – if the complaint is accepted, we'll fix the tattoo free of charge. Design changes are made before tattooing begins.

Who does the tattoos?

At SHADY TATTOO, tattoos are done by Shady (Shady Khabbaz, the studio owner), Ola and Zuzia. Martyna is the studio manager and performs the piercings.

Do you offer piercing?

Yes, SHADY TATTOO also offers piercing – ear piercing is done with the STUDEX System75. Ask Martyna for details when booking. More on the Piercing page.

How do I care for a fresh piercing?

Wash your hands before touching it, clean it with the product your piercer recommends (e.g. sterile saline), don't remove the jewellery, and avoid pools and saunas while it heals. If symptoms get worse, see a doctor.

How old do I need to be for a piercing?

In general, under Polish law a minor needs the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

What if my tattoo looks infected?

If you notice increasing pain, swelling, pus or a fever, contact a doctor straight away.
